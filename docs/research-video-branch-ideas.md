# The video (plan) branch: V_plan audit, evidence, and what is left to try

Research memo, 2026-10-15 (rewrite). Sources: `script/lingbot_rl_{model,policy,train,buffer,data,config}.py` at HEAD (differs from the results commit `e1dab88` only by the `--plan-ranker q` evaluation option and two replay metrics), `docs/heldout-results.md` ("Plan selection at the 5 / 10 sampler"), the offline studies `.superpowers/vplan/vplan_study.py`, `vplan_study2.py` and their raw output `study_results.jsonl`, the spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-plan-selection-design.md` §5–§9, and the papers cited by arXiv id. Summary: V_plan is a 10-head regression from a 6,144-d mean-pool pair onto the max over four candidates of the live min-ensemble Q; it ranks plans at 0.51–0.55 argmax agreement with Q on its own training rows and about 0.45 online, capturing 0.73–0.81 of the available plan value in-sample and roughly half online. Two independent halves of the Q ensemble agree with each other only 0.63 of the time and capture 0.88–0.90 of each other's value, so the target itself is noisy at the level V_plan is being judged against; every offline change to the loss or architecture scored below the recipe control on held-out rows. Plan selection with V_plan reached 79–80/100 on task 9 and 96/100 on task 0 (SFT 60 and 75), but no held-out run isolates V_plan's ranking from a random plan. The lever that remains is the quality and coverage of Q, not the fit of V_plan to it; the cheapest decisive experiment is the missing random-plan ablation.

## 1. Current V_plan setup and its issues

What it is, from the code.

- Inputs. `s` is `_pool_from_latent` of the isolated VAE encoding of the current frame (`lingbot_rl_policy.py:306–309`, `218–224`): one latent frame. `p_m` is `_pool_from_latent` of plan m's fully denoised 4-frame chunk latent (`:593`). `_pool_from_latent` (`:232–258`) runs one transformer pass at timestep 0, `frame_st_id` 0, against an empty critic cache (no history), captures the pre-`proj_out` video tokens, appends the text tokens from `condition_embedder.text_embedder(prompt_embeds)` and takes the token mean (`pool_critic_state`, `:31–34`). With 128×128 frames, two cameras width-concatenated and patch (1, 2, 2) the latent grid is 8×16, so 32 video tokens per latent frame: `p` averages 128 video tokens with 512 text tokens (the instruction zero-padded to 512 and embedded, constant for a run), `s` averages 32 with the same 512. Spatial and temporal structure is discarded; the informative part of the mean is 20% (plan) and 6% (state) of the averaged tokens, the rest a fixed offset. The critic state is `s ‖ p` (6,144-d, `lingbot_rl_model.py:137–140`); Q takes `s ‖ p ‖ a` (6,624-d, `:92`, `:123`).
- Architecture. `PlanCritic` (`:104–114`): 10 heads, each 6,144 → 1,024 → 1,024 → 1,024 → 1 with LayerNorm and GELU, about 8.4M parameters per head, 84M in all. Ranking is argmax over plans of the min over heads (`rank_plans`, `:152–155`).
- Target. `update_plan_critic` (`:234–254`), under `no_grad`: for every stored plan m and candidate k, `a = a_base_mk + actor(s, z_k, a_base_mk)` (the same K action noises for all plans, `:241`), `y_m = max_k min_j Q_j(s ‖ p_m, a)` with the live critic `self.critic` (`:244`), not the Polyak target. Loss is the sum over heads of MSE to `y_m`; Adam 1e-4, weight decay 1e-5, joint gradient clip 1.0, cosine-restart schedule. No target network, no bootstrap through V_plan. Gradients reach only V_plan.
- Cadence. `_update_from_buffer` (`lingbot_rl_train.py:254–272`): every 4 chunks, 10 gradient steps; each step draws 256 finalised rows at the RLPD expert ratio (0.9 → 0.1 over 208k env steps, `lingbot_rl_config.py:68–70`), updates Q, then updates V_plan on the same batch with the just-updated Q (`:267`, `:269`). Expert rows carry one pool (the next four true latent frames, `lingbot_rl_data.py:101–104`) repeated M times and the demo action repeated K × M times (`lingbot_rl_buffer.py:50`, `:52`, `:65–68`).
- Collection (`lingbot_rl_train.py:453–486`). Random plan for the first 64k env steps and with probability 0.1 afterwards (`np.random.random`, `:455`); otherwise V_plan's argmax. All M × K candidates are decoded (`decode_all=True`). The executed candidate is random during the warm-up, else the Q argmax within the chosen plan (`:470–474`). `plan_info` (agreement of V_plan's argmax with the argmax over plans of `max_k Q`, regret `max_m − Q[executed plan]`, V spread, random flag) is computed every chunk (`:483–486`) but logged only when an update runs (`:538–539`), i.e. for the last chunk of each 4-chunk window, with the pre-update critic.
- Evaluation (`lingbot_rl_policy.py:631–651`). V_plan picks the plan, K = 4 candidates of that plan are decoded, Q picks the candidate. `--plan-ranker q` decodes all 16 and takes Q's argmax (`rank_candidates`, `lingbot_rl_model.py:142–150`). No random plan at evaluation.
- Logged. `plan_critic_loss` (sum of 10 per-head MSEs), `v_plan_mean`, `plan_target_mean`; the four `plan_*` collection metrics above; every 5k env steps on a replay sample, `replay_plan_value_spread`, `replay_plan_delta_v` (V_plan of the executed plan minus its mean over plans, V_plan's own assessment, over rows chosen by older V_plans and random draws) and `replay_plan_delta_h` (`lingbot_rl_train.py:79–89`).

Issues. (D) marks a design choice taken knowingly, (X) a defect or inconsistency.

1. (D) All ten heads see identical rows, inputs and targets and differ only in initialisation (`lingbot_rl_model.py:107`, `:245–247`; `lingbot_rl_train.py:269`), as do Q's heads (`:92`, `:172–173`). The min over heads therefore does not average independent errors; the split-half measurement in §2 shows how large the initialisation variance alone is.
2. (D) The target is the max over four noisy values of a critic that changes every gradient step (`:244`, after `:267`); the max is biased upward on near-ties. The Polyak-target refit was no better (test captured 0.59 vs 0.62), so this is not the bottleneck.
3. (D) The pool discards spatial and temporal structure, and most of the averaged tokens are a constant text offset (above). The first LayerNorm absorbs the offset, so the cost is scale rather than information, but the plan is represented by 128 tokens' mean.
4. (X) Expert rows contribute M = 4 identical (s, p, y) copies (`lingbot_rl_buffer.py:50`, `:52`), so each demo chunk weighs four times what spec §7 states ("expert rows one") while matching §9. With the expert ratio at 0.9 → 0.28 over the first 160k steps, 28–90% of every V_plan batch carries no ranking information. Harmless for ranking, wasteful, and the spec contradicts itself.
5. (D) No held-out split, early stopping or validation online. The only out-of-sample signal during a run is `plan_regret`/`plan_agreement`, sampled one chunk in four and mixed with the 10% random chunks (`:538`), which overstate ranked-chunk regret by about 0.1 × 0.075.
6. (X) Whether a chunk's plan was random is not stored (`:527–528` store `plan_index` only), so random-plan chunks cannot be identified in a saved buffer. `vplan_study2.py:38–41` computes its return split over all online rows, not "on the random-plan chunks" as `docs/heldout-results.md` states; see §2.
7. (D) Q's bootstrap uses only the executed plan's next pool `p′` (`lingbot_rl_buffer.py:118`; `lingbot_rl_model.py:163–168`), averaged over the K next candidates. Q(s, p, a) is the value of this plan under the behaviour's future plan choices, not under the best future plan, and it under-predicts success by about 0.19 (`docs/heldout-results.md`, BC-filter section). V_plan inherits both properties.
8. (D) The random plan, buffer sampling and task choice share the global `np.random` stream (`lingbot_rl_policy.py:574`; `lingbot_rl_train.py:455`, `:440`; `lingbot_rl_buffer.py:134–136`), saved and restored in the resume file. Reproducible, but any change in the number of draws reshuffles all three.
9. (D) Capacity versus data: 84M parameters fit 41k targets at 160k env steps (10,278 states × 4). The target's within-state std is 0.073 against 0.40 across states (`vplan_study.py` output), so MSE spends most of its fit on the state level, which does not affect ranking; the state-centred loss that removes it overfit worse (test captured 0.41).
10. (X) Spec divergence: §6 says the plan metrics are logged per chunk (code: one in four); §7 says expert rows contribute one plan (code and §9: M copies). Everything else in §5–§9 matches the code, including the live-Q target, one V_plan step per Q step, min-over-heads ranking, and the executed-plan bootstrap.

Held-out, 5 / 10 sampler, 100 episodes per policy (50 canonical init states × 2 seeds), from `docs/heldout-results.md`:

|---|---|---|---|

| 9 | plan selection (V_plan) + residual, 160k | 79 | 30 / 11 |
| 9 | same checkpoint, plan by Q over all 16 candidates | 74 | 30 / 16; 14 / 19 vs V_plan |
| 9 | plan selection (V_plan) + residual, 320k | 80 | 32 / 12; 13 / 12 vs its own 160k |
| 0 | SFT step 600 | 75 | — |
| 0 | plan selection (V_plan) + residual, 240k | 96 | 23 / 2 |

Episode wall time on a GB10: 65 s SFT, 120–130 s at M = 4 with V_plan, 190 s with Q over 16. Chunk cost at M = 4: about 6.7 s, 1.1 s per plan, 1.9 s at M = 1.

Not run: M = 4 with a random plan (same residual and Q), and M = 1 at the 5 / 10 sampler. The 19–21 point gains over SFT therefore bundle the residual, candidate selection and plan selection, and "V_plan beats a random plan" on held-out episodes is not established; it is inferred from the collection regret below and from V_plan beating the Q ranker (74 vs 79, 14 / 19 paired, inside the ±0.09 interval). The second half of training added nothing measurable (79 → 80, 13 / 12 paired).

Training signals, task 9 (`lsy1uiuu`), window means: agreement 0.48 during the random-plan warm-up, 0.41–0.49 for the rest of the run, never rising; regret 0.082 under random plans, 0.036–0.045 once V_plan ranked; `replay_plan_delta_v` 0.007 → 0.038; collection success 0.55 → 0.71–0.82. Task 0: regret 0.039 → 0.019, agreement 0.44–0.53. The flat agreement over 250k env steps means more data of the same kind did not improve V_plan's fit to Q out of sample.

Offline refits (`vplan_study.py`, `vplan_study2.py`; targets recomputed with the final critic for every stored plan; "captured" = 1 − regret / random-plan regret; the 80 / 20 split is by row order, so the test rows are the latest 20%). The live V_plan was trained on all rows, so its "test" column is in-sample; the fresh fits are held out.

The bagged, early-stopped variants in `vplan_study2.py:106–107` have no recorded output. 160k buffer facts: 10,278 online states, within-state target spread 0.16, random-plan regret 0.075, executed-plan regret 0.039 (over all rows including the roughly 45% random-plan rows).

Critic self-consistency (`vplan_study2.py:30–37`): ranking the stored plans with Q heads 1–5 and heads 6–10 separately, the halves agree on the argmax 0.63 of the time (160k and 320k) and each captures 0.88 (160k) / 0.90 (320k) of the value under the other; the full ensemble agrees with one half 0.80–0.82, inflated by the shared heads. The return split (0.67 / 0.70 mean Monte Carlo return when the executed plan is the final critic's argmax, 0.37 / 0.39 otherwise) is over all rows, where "executed = argmax" is enriched for later, better chunks and for rows whose return Q was trained on; it does not test Q's ranking against realised value.

What the self-consistency implies. Any ranker regressed onto this Q is scored against one draw of the ten heads. An independent draw of the same critic, which is the best proxy for "perfect fit to the expected target", agrees with that draw 0.63 and captures 0.88–0.90 of its value. The live V_plan, in-sample, captures 0.73 (160k) and 0.81 (320k), 83–90% of that ceiling, with agreement 0.08–0.12 below it. Online, removing the random chunks' share from the logged regret (0.040 − 0.1 × 0.075)/0.9 ≈ 0.036 gives captured ≈ 1 − 0.036/0.075 ≈ 0.5 and agreement ≈ 0.49 if the random-plan regret stayed near 0.075: about 55% of the ceiling. So there is out-of-sample headroom against Q, but the offline study found no loss or architecture that reached even the recipe control on held-out rows, and the online agreement did not move with 2.5× more data. The ceiling against the true value is lower than the ceiling against Q by Q's own error, of which split-half measures only the initialisation variance, not the bias shared by all heads (the under-prediction, the behaviour-plan bootstrap, coverage).

Gaussian noise model (a model, not a measurement): four plans with true values N(0, σ_v²), independent Gaussian errors; max − min of four is 2.06 σ_v, so σ_v ≈ 0.16/2.06 ≈ 0.078.

| error / σ_v | agreement with truth | captured under truth | two such observers: agreement, mutual captured |

The split-half pair (0.63, 0.88) does not fit one row: 0.63 agreement implies error ≈ 0.55 σ_v and captured 0.77, while 0.88 captured implies error ≈ 0.4 σ_v and agreement 0.71. The errors are not iid Gaussian; the plans contain many near-ties that the halves rank differently at little cost in value and a few clear winners that both find. Captured value is the quantity to track; agreement overstates the loss.

## 3. Literature, condensed to what still applies

Noise-space RL for the video branch (DSRL, 2506.15799). Mechanism: a small actor outputs the initial video noise, the frozen sampler is part of the environment, a noise critic is regressed onto the decoded result's value. Evidence: π0 on LIBERO about 20% → about 100% in about 10k samples, but with a 32-d noise actor repeated across the chunk; nobody has trained one above about 1.6k noise dimensions; steering is bounded by base coverage (OGPO 2605.03065). Related: GoldenStart (amortised best-of-N over noises), Noise PPO 2506.12036 (full-dimensional noise policy, 0.2–0.3 points), Golden Ticket 2603.15757 (one fixed noise beat sampling on 38/43 tasks, none over a whole suite), noise hypernetworks 2508.09968. Fit: our video noise is 48 × 4 × 8 × 16 ≈ 25k-d per plan; a 192-d per-channel-per-frame shift is the DSRL-style parametrisation. Cost: inference at M = 1 (1.1 s per chunk); training data comes from collection. Risk: the 0.16 value gap between plans may live in local noise structure a low-dimensional actor cannot reach, and the regression target is V_plan, whose online fidelity to Q is about 0.5 captured. Status: untested; only worth an offline R² check of (s, structured noise) → V_plan on the stored seeds, and only after the ranker question in §4 is settled.

Inference-time noise search with a verifier (FASTER, 2604.19730; Ma et al. 2501.09732; ReNO 2406.04312; Diffusion-ES 2402.06559). Evidence: at equal sample count structured search did not beat random best-of-N (Ma et al.); FASTER's noise-level critic scores N = 8 noises before denoising and reaches best-of-N parity on robomimic and π0.5 LIBERO-90 at about half the compute, with a 300k-parameter critic. Fit: a V_noise(s, w) regressed onto V_plan over the stored seeds (4 per state), then screen 16–32 free noises and denoise the top 2–4. Cost: halves the chunk at M = 2, or doubles the effective plan count at today's cost. Risk: learnability of a 25k-d noise → value map from 10k states is unknown (FASTER's noises are 1–2k-d), and the final choice still rests on V_plan. Status: the cheapest experiment here and the only one that raises the number of plans per state without more video passes; speculative until the offline R² exists.

Reward fine-tuning of the video branch (Flow-GRPO 2505.05470, DanceGRPO 2505.07818, DDPO 2305.13301, DRaFT 2309.17400, Diffusion-DPO 2311.12908, Flow-DPO 2501.13918, πRL 2510.25889, ReinFlow 2505.22094). Evidence: works at LoRA scale with a KL anchor and thousands of prompts; collapses in diversity or hacks the reward without the anchor; Gao et al. 2210.10760 put a reward model under about 2k comparisons at chance. Fit: the reward would be V_plan or max_k Q from 10k states, and §2 shows that reward is at best 0.5–0.8 faithful to a critic that is itself noisy. Cost: about 2 s per LoRA update on a GB10, plus SDE or likelihood machinery on our Euler sampler. Risk: moves plans off Q's support, loses the best-of-4 diversity that produces the gain, undetectable without 6 h of held-out evaluation. Status: not supported by the evidence; revisit only if a trusted plan value exists.

Value-guided sampling of the plan (CGPO 2605.30056, Guided Action Flow 2607.02092, Flow-NRG 2501.13918). Evidence: GAF 60 → 82.5% on a 0.45B VLA with no best-of-N comparison; CGPO never deploys guidance at inference; Flow-NRG shows hacking at inference; Ma et al. found first-order search no better than zero-order. Fit: the gradient of an 84M-parameter regressor on a 6,144-d mean pool fitted to 41k targets, which every refit overfit. Cost: about +15% per chunk if only the last pass is guided, 2× if all are. Status: not recommended; the gradient-free re-noise mutation of the FASTER item is the in-distribution version.

Video-world-model planning with a value (VLP 2310.10625, UniPi 2302.00111, VPP 2412.14803, GR-2 2410.06158, DINO-WM 2411.04983, ViVa 2604.08168, World Value Model 2606.24742, World-Value-Action 2604.14732). Evidence: VLP is the only one that quantifies selection against budget and it clips its value model because search exploits it; ViVa/WVM report that value heads on the video model's own features beat external value models on physical judgement. Fit: our V_plan is an external MLP on an output mean pool; the in-model alternative is the action branch's cross-attention readout of the plan, available during the decode we already run. Status: the representation idea is plausible and untested; nothing else transfers.

## 4. The ranker, given the ceiling

Loss and architecture changes are not supported. On held-out rows every alternative (Polyak target, state-centred MSE, pairwise ranking, pool-minus-state features, narrow regularised heads, HL-Gauss on the max or on all 16 targets) captured 0.41–0.59 against the recipe control's 0.60–0.62 at 160k and 0.43–0.46 against 0.49 at 320k. The control's own held-out capture is below the live ranker's in-sample 0.73–0.81, which is within 0.07–0.17 of what an independent draw of Q captures (0.88–0.90). Fitting V_plan more tightly to Q therefore has at most 0.1–0.15 of captured value to gain in-sample, and the one online quantity that would show it, agreement, stayed at 0.41–0.49 for 250k env steps.

What remains plausible, with the honest expected effect:

1. The random-plan ablation first. Evaluate the 160k task-9 checkpoint with M = 4 and a random plan, same residual and Q, 100 episodes (about 3.5 h on a GB10), and M = 1 at the 5 / 10 sampler. This is the only measurement that attributes any of the 19 points to V_plan's ranking. If random-plan M = 4 lands within the interval of 79, the ranker is not where the gain is and the rest of this section is moot.
2. Improve Q rather than V_plan. Q's heads share all data; heads trained on disjoint row subsets (bootstrapped ensembles, Osband et al. 1602.04621; conservative combination, Coste et al. 2310.02743) make the min average independent errors instead of initialisation noise. Expected effect: raises the split-half ceiling by an unknown amount; it does not touch the shared bias from the behaviour-plan bootstrap (issue 7) or the 0.19 under-prediction. Measurable offline on the saved buffers before any run: retrain Q heads on folds and repeat the split-half measurement. Speculative.
3. More plans per state at low cost. The within-state target std (0.073) is comparable to the per-head error, so the ranking signal is near the noise floor at M = 4. M = 8 doubles video time; the FASTER-style noise pre-screen or a re-noise mutation of the stored winner (σ ≤ 0.5, two passes, about 0.4 s) adds in-distribution plans with Q targets at a fraction of the cost (Cobbe et al. 2110.14168 and Gao et al.: unique samples, not epochs, move a verifier). Expected effect: more targets per state for V_plan and a wider best-of-N at inference; bounded by Q's accuracy on plans it has not seen executed. Untested.
4. In-model plan features. Per-frame pools or the action branch's readout of the plan through a learned 128–256-d projection with LayerNorm (BRO 2405.16158; ViVa/WVM above), fixed after a one-off predictive fit so it cannot drift. Expected effect: the refit study says the 6,144-d input with 41k targets overfits within 4k steps; a lower-dimensional input is the direct answer, but no refit so far has beaten the control, so treat this as a hypothesis with a cheap offline test (same 80 / 20 protocol) and no held-out expectation.

Not recommended now: changing V_plan's loss (MSE, pairwise, HL-Gauss, centred, all measured), a target network for V_plan (measured), gradient guidance of the plan, any fine-tuning of the video model against V_plan or Q, and a full-dimensional noise actor.

## 5. Three papers to read in full

- FASTER, 2604.19730: noise-level critic matching best-of-N at a fraction of the compute, including on a 3.3B VLA.
- Osband et al., "Deep Exploration via Bootstrapped DQN", 1602.04621, with Coste et al. 2310.02743 for conservative combination: ensembles whose heads see different data.
- Farebrother et al., "Stop Regressing", 2403.03950: why classification losses help value regression under noisy targets, and why they did not help here (our refits overfit before the loss mattered).
